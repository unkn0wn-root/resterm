# Response History and Diffing

The History tab lets you filter, preview, replay, and delete saved runs.

- `/` filters and `Enter` loads the selected entry.
- `p` previews and `r` replays.
- `Space` selects entries and `d` deletes the selection.
- `c` changes scope and `s` changes sort order.

The Diff tab compares the focused response pane against the pinned one. Pin a pane with `Ctrl+Shift+V`.
